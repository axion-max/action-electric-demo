Electrical Service FAQs

Here you'll find answers to some of the most common questions we receive about electrician services. Whether you're a homeowner or a property manager, Action Electric Co. of Houston Midtown provides clear, informative answers to help you better understand your electrical system.

For general safety, we recommend having your electrical system inspected by our service professionals every 3-5 years. You should also schedule an inspection if: Your home is more than 40 years old, you're buying a new home, you're planning a major renovation, or you're adding major new appliances.
While changing a lightbulb is simple, most electrical repairs should be left to a professional. DIY electrical work can be life threatening and may lead to electric shock, fire, or damage to your appliances. It can also violate local building codes and void your homeowner's insurance. For anything beyond the most basic tasks, it's always safest to call a licensed electrician.
A Ground Fault Circuit Interrupter (GFCI) outlet is a special type of outlet that quickly shuts off power if it detects an imbalance in the current. This rapid shutdown helps prevent serious electric shock. GFCI outlets are required by code in areas where moisture is present, such as bathrooms, kitchens, basements, and garages.
We can help boost your energy savings. Some of the best ways to upgrade your electrical components to save energy and money include: Switch to LED Lighting, Install Dimmers, Use Smart Plugs and Switches, Consider a Whole-Home Surge Protector.
We can inspect your home for potential electrical upgrade needs. Your home might need electrical upgrades if you notice any of the following signs: Frequently tripping breakers or blowing fuses, flickering or dimming lights, outlets that are warm to the touch, burning smells, or if your home is over 40 years old.
An electrical safety inspection can reveal the risk of serious damage to your property and harm to your loved ones. Mr. Electric can perform a safety inspection to ensure your system operates correctly. Our safety reviews check several points including grounding systems, wire outlets, and HVAC systems.
During a natural disaster or unexpected power outage, a generator makes all the difference. It can keep your belongings and family safe at home, or ensure your business operations don't come to a halt and risk the well-being of your employees. We offer many electrical services in Houston Midtown, TX, including generator installation.
